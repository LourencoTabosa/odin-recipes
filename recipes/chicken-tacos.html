Chicken Tacos

Home

This slow cooker chicken tacos recipe is easy to make with just 4 ingredients for flavorful, super tender, shredded chicken. Spoon the chicken into warm tortillas for a very tasty meal any day of the week.

Ingredients

  • Broth
  • Taco seasoning
  • Chicken
  • Corn tortillas

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients
  2. Combine chicken broth and taco seasoning mix in a bowl.
  3. Place chicken in a slow cooker. Pour chicken broth mixture over chicken.
  4. Cover slow cooker. Cook on Low for 6 to 8 hours. Shred chicken with two forks.
  5. Warm corn tortillas in a pan
  6. Serve shredded chicken in warmed corn tortillas
  7. Enjoy it!
